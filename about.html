About

d3n4c3 is my personal site — a place to share projects I'm building, thoughts I want to capture, and whatever else feels worth putting on the page.

Some posts will be deep dives into things I'm working on; others might be shorter notes, experiments, or half-formed ideas. The goal is simple: keep a public record of what I'm making and thinking, in one spot that's easy to browse and search.

Use the menu to explore posts by category, search the archives from the header, or check the featured post on the home page for the latest welcome note.

Hamilton-style magazines (Baxter Fanfare portfolios)

  • Marvel Fanfare is the home format for Hamilton’s sleek pin-ups. Tearoom-archived portfolios + CBR indexes show only two pre-1994 Fanfare Phoenix pin-ups:
  • Fanfare #18 (Jan 1985) — Kevin Nowlan Phoenix (SBS #72): same Baxter portfolio look (sleek line, high contrast). Flying with both arms raised / flaming V-wings — pose miss.
  • Fanfare #35 (Nov 1987) — Craig Hamilton Phoenix (SBS #02 / #71): arms-across / hand-to-brow — style cousin, pose miss. Rest of that portfolio has no second Jean.
  • Other Fanfare portfolios checked (Austin, Steacy, Breyfogle, Brigman, Adams/Golden/BWS mix, etc.): no additional Phoenix pin-ups. Fanfare #40 Hamilton story = Storm vs Mystique (no Jean).
  • Related print: 1986 Marvel Press Poster #24 Leonardi/Austin Phoenix (already SBS #10; Comics Feature B&W reprint) — both arms raised. Pose miss.

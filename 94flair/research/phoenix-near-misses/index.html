Craig Hamilton deep-dive

  • Best Hamilton pose cousin (near-exact arms+legs): CAF #286213 “Craig Hamilton – Dark Phoenix” (H. Amano gallery) — left arm forward, right fist at hip, same floating leg tuck as PB#5. Signed 1.4.06, so it post-dates 1994 Flair. Likely Hamilton echoing the Flair silhouette (or redrawing a lost earlier sketch). Not installable as SOURCE_ART without a pre-1994 twin.
  • Fanfare #35 (Nov 1987) 6-page Hamilton portfolio: only one Phoenix — arms-across / hand-to-brow pin-up (already SBS #02 / #71). Style cousin, pose miss. Portfolio also: Nightcrawler, Scarlet Witch/Vision, Cloak & Dagger, Colossus, Kitty/Lockheed — no second Jean.
  • Fanfare #40 Hamilton X-Men story is Storm vs Mystique (no Jean); Doctor Strange/Clea pin-up only.
  • Other archived Hamilton Phoenix sketches checked via CAF CDN: pencil pinup #148194 (’05, arms raised), Trophy Wall #163625 (seated), Painting #173861 (arms-up V), 4C Dark Phoenix #2007250 (2023, claw hands). All pose misses.

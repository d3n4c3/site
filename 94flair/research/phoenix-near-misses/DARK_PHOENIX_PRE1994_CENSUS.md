# Dark Phoenix / Classic Phoenix — Pre–Summer 1994 Visual Census

**Cutoff:** before Flair Marvel Annual **Powerblast** set (~**June 1994**).  
**Focus:** Jean Grey Earth-616 **classic green Phoenix** (firebird + gold sash) or **black/red Dark Phoenix** (firebird + gold sash). Full-figure / focus art preferred (covers, splashes, pin-ups, posters, cards, model sheets, portfolios). Tiny cameos skipped unless asymmetric blast is clear.  
**PB5 target pose:** flying/floating; **LEFT** arm extended **palm blast toward viewer**; **RIGHT fist clenched at hip**. Costume color ignored for verdict.  
**Verdict key:** `MATCH` · `STRONG COUSIN` · `WEAK COUSIN` · `MISS` · `UNKNOWN` (need scan) · `POST-1994 ECHO`

**Status (2026-03):** No pre-1994 print `MATCH` found. Best pre-1994 pose cousins are **1992 Impel X-Men #24 (Jim Lee)** and **1992 Masterpieces #46 (Jusko)** — both Lee-era Jean costumes, not classic DP/Phoenix suit. Hamilton CAF Amano pencil (2006) is a near-exact pose echo but **too late**.

**Already SBS?** refers to local Powerblast #5 side-by-sides under `94flair/research/phoenix-near-misses/hq-sbs/` (source-hunt branch).

## Method notes

- **Do not invent:** rows without a verified scan are `UNKNOWN`.  
- **Costume vs pose:** STRONG COUSINs #47–49 wear Lee-era Jean suits; they remain the best *pose* leads even though they fail the classic DP/Phoenix costume filter.  
- **Concurrent 1994 cards** (#60–63) listed for completeness; they cannot be Flair sources if they shipped the same season.  
- Reverse-image (prior wave): Yandex on live PB5 HD returned only Flair variants — no earlier comic/pin-up indexed.

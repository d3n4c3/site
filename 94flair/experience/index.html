Flair ’94: Hands On

About

Marvel and Fleer, 1994: 150 cards, each a milestone in Marvel history. Scroll to deal; the cards are yours to hold.

Base set · 150

Thick stock, double UV coated and foil stamped on both sides.

How the art was made

Marvel’s Special Projects team sent previously published black line art, with color guides, to McGrew Color Graphics, which painted it on computer.

Powerblasts · 18

The chase set: prismatic, metallic-enhanced cards, about one in every two packs. Marvel Age had promised 20; eighteen shipped.

Flairprints & binder inserts

Flairprints were 6½″ × 10″ case bonuses, almost three cards tall, reusing Powerblast #1–10 art. The five binder inserts reuse Powerblast #11–15 art with no logo, no title and no foil.

Promos & promo sheets

Deadpool and Omega Red promo Flairprints went to the trade. Double-sided 5″ × 7″ 4-card sheets came bagged with Wizard #35, Advance Comics #67, Marvel Age #138 and Tuff Stuff’s Collect!. Nine uncut 7⅝″ × 10⅝″ 9-card sheets came in Fleer’s 1994 media press kit, never sold in packs.

Release timeline · 1994

Scroll to move through the year, one piece at a time.

  • May 1994: The first indexed ad: Fantastic Four #388 carries the newsprint “Inaugural Edition” ad.
  • May 1994: The glossy magazine ad, with Powerblast art, runs in Tuff Stuff’s Collect! the same month.
  • June 1994: Tuff Stuff’s Collect! comes with a 4-card promo sheet. “Ships June 1994” is printed on the sheets.
  • June 11–14, 1994: Omega Red vs. Wolverine promo Flairprint, handed out at Diamond’s Annual Retailer Seminar in Baltimore.
  • July 1994: Marvel Age #138 runs the preview, “Where the Past and Future Collide,” with a 4-card sheet in the bag.
  • July 1994: Pages 26–27: Marvel and Fleer reveal that every card is previously published art, freshly colored on computer.
  • July 1994: Wizard #35 is polybagged with a sheet reported to be the gold-foil etched version.
  • July 1994: Advance Comics #67 comes with a 4-card sheet.
  • July 1994: Hero Illustrated #13 carries the separate Flairprints ad.

Free play

Each Deal opens one pack of 10 cards, base cards unique within the pack.

Oversized Cards

Flairprints & Binder

Two different oversized releases that often get lumped together. Flairprints were the case bonus packed with the original Flair set. Binder inserts showed up in collector binders (exact release window is less certain). Both reuse Powerblast art, so each card carries the same source and artist credits as its Powerblast.

How to tell them apart

Flip it over: a Flairprint back carries the UPC, while a binder insert back is completely blank. On the front, both reuse Powerblast art, but a Flairprint keeps the Flair '94 logo and character name while a binder insert has neither and drops the metallic finish.

Promo Flairprints 1994

Oversized Flairprint-style promos sent out through comic distributors ahead of the release.

4-Card Uncut Sheets Jun–Jul 1994

Double-sided 5″ × 7″ panels. Released versions: front has the Flair logo, Bone Claws, Phoenix, Maximum Carnage. Back has Bodyslide, Spider-Man 2099, Thunderstrike, New Ghost Rider. They came bundled with comic magazines (see Ads & Magazines). The gold-foil etched version is reported as the Wizard #35 insert. The other magazine and variant pairings are unknown.

9-Card Uncut Sheets 7⅝″ × 10⅝″ · Card #s left→right, top→bottom

These came in Fleer's 1994 media press kit: a Wolverine folder holding the uncut sheets, a couple of single cards (Blade and Terrax), and press documents on Marvel letterhead with a history of Marvel and a character timeline. They were preview material, never sold in packs. Which sheets came in which kit isn't documented.

Missing #6: numbering error — no card #6 exists. Two #8s instead: Iron Man (the real #6) and Vulture vs. Spider-Man.

4-Card Sheet Variants

Two known variants: "Ships June 1994" in white letters etched in gold foil, and the same text in black on a gold paste-on sticker. Bundled with Wizard #35, Advance Comics #67, Marvel Age #138 (all Jul 1994), and Tuff Stuff's Collect! (Jun 1994).

An unreleased sheet (Gambit & Rogue, Infinity Crusade, Maximum Carnage, Bone Claws) turned up on eBay — no front text; back fourth bullet says "presents" instead of "present," headed "The Ultimate Collectable."

Promos & Binder Cards

Diamond's Omega Red vs. Wolverine Flairprint: Annual Retailer Seminar, Baltimore, June 11–14, 1994. Deadpool Flairprint: Heroes World Con 1994 (nslists; no published day range).

Binder inserts are oversized like Flairprints: Powerblast art without metallic finish, no Flair '94 logo/title, blank backs. Spider-Man binder verified in sealed '95 Fleer Ultra X-Men binders (2–3 per binder); some may also have been issued promotionally. Exact release timing isn't locked down.

Hobby Box & Packs

Each hobby box held 24 packs of 10 cards. Packs shipped as thick cardboard mini-boxes with character foil art on the front — four cover variants (Venom, Magneto, Cyclops, Wolverine), shrink-wrapped, not a conventional wax wrapper. Powerblast chase landed about 1:2 packs; Flairprints were a case bonus, not in the packs.

About the Art Credits

Most Flair cards reuse existing comic art, repainted for the set. Each card lists its front title, source, and artist. Blank credits aren't identified yet. Research by @exotechktx.

Likely unused in print: #10 Xavier, #42 Elektra, #44 Hobgoblin, #85 Bishop, Powerblast #5 Phoenix. #124 Bloodhawk source unconfirmed.
